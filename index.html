About the artist / Mokattam, Cairo

Meet the artist behind the marks.

Farida Ibrahim is a Cairo, Egypt-based artist. Born on October 28, 1996, she studied fine arts in the painting department at Helwan University.

Farida has participated in 15 group exhibitions in different cities across Egypt. She has also taken part in recycling projects for sustainable art and human development initiatives, and volunteered as an art trainer for orphan children through the Ministry of Youth and Sports.

Every work begins with looking. Some observations become places.
